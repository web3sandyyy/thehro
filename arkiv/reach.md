# Reach

Target users: people comparing small or medium USDC positions across DeFi venues, especially when gas and bridging costs are material relative to the incremental yield.

Entry point: compare an illustrative scenario without a wallet, then select a pool from the live DeFiLlama feed. A user can enter their own principal, current rate, holding period and estimated all-in cost.

Potential reuse: a wallet, portfolio tool or independent frontend could read the same owner-scoped schema and reproduce the scenario with its own calculator. JSON export preserves the inputs locally before expiry.

No adoption numbers are claimed. The application has not undergone user testing. Proposed next validation: ask five hackathon participants to compare a 30-day and 90-day hold and explain why the conclusion differs.
